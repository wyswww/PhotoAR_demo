# PhotoAR 界面与图标

界面采用深海蓝半透明卡片、青绿色主按钮与清晰中文层级。相机画面作为主要工作区，底部控制台可收起；独立开始/停止自转按钮与状态提示避免误操作。模型库展示电脑上的生成时间、文件大小和照片缩略图，点击记录即可加载并重新定位。

图标文件：AppIcon.png。采用内置 image_gen 生成，原样复制到 Unity/Assets/PhotoAR/Resources/AppIcon.png，配置默认、Android Legacy、Round 和 Adaptive 图标。没有对生成图进行二次绘制。Android Adaptive 图标使用深蓝背景与生成图前景。

生成提示词：

Create a polished square Android app icon for a photo-to-3D augmented reality app named PhotoAR. No text or lettering. Edge-to-edge deep midnight navy background, subtle radial gradient. Centered luminous translucent teal and cyan isometric cube, with one clear white cyan camera-viewfinder corner motif wrapping around it and a subtle violet back facet, expressing a photographed object becoming three dimensional. Restrained premium modern product design, crisp bold silhouette recognizable at 48px, balanced geometric composition, soft controlled highlights, no tiny details, no watermark. Keep the symbol within the center 60 percent to survive Android adaptive circular and squircle masks. Square flat icon artwork, no phone mockup, no surrounding border or external drop shadow, opaque background.

字体采用 Noto Sans CJK SC，许可见 Noto-OFL.txt；官方来源 https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/main/Sans/OTF/SimplifiedChinese 。

EasyAR 个人测试授权自带的水印保持原样。
